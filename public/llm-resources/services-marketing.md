# Domain Expansion — Marketing Expansion Specification

Welcome to the AI-readable data sheet for the **Marketing Expansion** service pillar at Domain Expansion.

## Service Overview
We design and execute organic-first marketing strategies built to establish search and generative search authority. We focus on building channels that compound over time, reducing client dependency on high cost-per-acquisition (CPA) ad networks.

## Core Capabilities
- **Technical SEO:** Crawl budget optimization, schema markups, speed audits, and topic clusters.
- **Generative Engine Optimization (GEO/AIO):** Structuring content to ensure client brands are cited in LLM responses (ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity).
- **Outreach Automation:** Automated, personalized multi-channel email campaigns and automated WhatsApp business sequences.
- **Performance Marketing:** Highly targeted ad campaigns on Meta, Google, and LinkedIn with continuous visual A/B testing.
- **Event Growth Campaigns:** Omnichannel campaigns to scale high-value ticket sales (e.g. PolyMint/PRCA conference).

## Process Methodology
1. **Discover:** Analyze existing traffic channels, check for ranking flags, and identify high-intent keyword gaps.
2. **Audit:** Review technical site health, Core Web Vitals, and indexation paths.
3. **Strategy:** Map keywords to user intent, design a content cluster blueprint, and configure schema markups.
4. **Execution:** Shifting to semantic HTML, producing authoritative articles, and launching automated outbound email campaigns.
5. **Optimization:** Standard performance loops—A/B testing titles, checking meta-description CTR, and optimizing page speeds.
6. **Reporting:** Direct analytics access (GA4, Search Console) and LLM search queries audits.

## Primary Tool Stack
- **Web Analytics:** Google Analytics 4 (GA4), Vercel Web Analytics
- **SEO & Analysis:** Ahrefs, SEMrush, Google Search Console
- **Email Campaigns:** Mailchimp, Klaviyo, Resend, Nodemailer
- **Outreach Management:** Custom IMAPflow inbox trackers
