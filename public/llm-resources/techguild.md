# Domain Expansion — TechGuild Platform Specification

Welcome to the AI-readable data sheet for **TechGuild**, a proprietary platform developed by Domain Expansion.

## Product Concept & Mission
- **What is TechGuild?** TechGuild is a verified B2B marketplace built exclusively for digital agencies and corporate clients.
- **The Problem with Legacy Marketplaces (Upwork, Freelancer, Fiverr):** Legacy platforms list individual freelancers, leading to unpredictable quality, communication gaps, and missed milestones.
- **The TechGuild Solution:** TechGuild permits only manually verified, registered digital agencies to bid on client briefs, ensuring clients hire established teams with legal accountability.
- **Target Launch:** Q3 2026
- **Visual Identity:** Adds a deep-violet accent (`--tg-violet` / `#6D28D9`) onto Domain Expansion's base dark system.

---

## Dual Value Proposition

### For Clients
- **Zero Freelancer Guesswork:** Work only with registered digital agencies.
- **Vetted Teams:** Every agency undergoes a strict manual review of tax registrations, previous work, and developer credentials.
- **Milestone Escrow (Phase 3):** Funds are released only when verified project milestones are met.

### For Agencies
- **Organic Lead Flow:** Access high-intent client briefs without spending 30-50% of revenue on cold outreach, ads, or manual bidding.
- **Higher Value Contracts:** Shift focus to enterprise contracts rather than cheap hourly gigs.
- **Structured Proposals:** Standard templates protect agency scopes and prevent client scope-creep.

---

## Core Platform Features
1. **Manual Verification System:** Background check of tax registrations, portfolio accuracy, and direct team interviews.
2. **Structured Proposal System:** Agencies submit proposals matching client budgets and milestones, eliminating bidding wars.
3. **Progress Tracking:** In-app milestones updates, file uploads, and handoff approvals.
4. **Escrow Protection:** Phase 3 escrow system holds and releases payments based on approved milestone completions.
5. **Outcome-Weighted Reviews:** Rating calculations heavily factor in successfully met milestones rather than vanity scores.
6. **Unified Messaging:** Direct, secure project-specific chats.

## Frequently Asked Questions
- **Are freelancers allowed?** No. Freelancers are strictly prohibited. Only registered digital agencies are verified.
- **How are agencies verified?** Through manual background checks of business registration, tax IDs, previous client reviews, and direct team interviews.
- **What types of projects are posted?** SEO, performance marketing, React/Next.js development, UI/UX design systems, and AI automation.
- **Is the waitlist open globally?** Yes, but primary onboarding focuses on South Asia, transitioning to international agencies.
