## Case Study 1: PolyMint / PRCA (7th Polymer Recycling Conference Asia)
- **Sector:** B2B Events & Professional Networking
- **Focus:** Omnichannel Digital Marketing & AI Lead Qualification
- **Campaign Duration:** 4 Months
- **Media Budget:** ₹20 Lakhs total (₹5 Lakhs per month)

### The Challenge
PolyMint needed to drive premium delegate ticket sales (priced at ₹20,000 per person) for their Delhi-based regional summit. The goal was to secure 500+ C-suite registrations, 30+ award nominations, and corporate sponsorships from Indian and international regions.

### Strategy & Execution
Domain Expansion structured a multi-channel campaign targeting recycling and environmental decision-makers:
- **Paid Ads:** Deployed targeted creative campaigns across LinkedIn, Meta, and Google.
- **AI Automation:** Set up automated cold outbound email campaigns and targeted WhatsApp marketing.
- **Lead Qualification:** Deployed AI calling bots to parse, qualify, and route leads, generating over 3 Million digital touchpoints.

### Results
- **Tickets Sold:** 670+ paid delegate registrations (exceeding the 500 target).
- **Total Leads:** 46,000+ total marketing leads.
- **Genuine Leads:** 2,700+ highly-interested leads delivered.
- **Sponsorships:** Secured 20+ corporate sponsors.
- **Nominations:** 40+ award nominations registered.

## Case Study 2: Data-Hat AI (SaaS Launch Organic Traffic)
- **Sector:** AI SaaS & Technology Startup (US-based)
- **Focus:** Technical SEO, Generative Engine Optimization (GEO), Content Strategy

### The Challenge
The client had near-zero organic search presence and relied completely on paid advertising, causing customer acquisition costs (CAC) to rise. They needed a compounding organic traffic channel to establish brand authority.

### Strategy & Execution
- **Web Speed:** Rebuilt the marketing site on a fast, standalone Next.js framework (LCP < 2s).
- **Semantics:** Deployed schema markups and semantic HTML tags.
- **Content:** Researched developer bottlenecks and published 30 authoritative articles organized in topic clusters.
- **GEO Integration:** Structured page entities to trigger citations in LLM search queries (ChatGPT, Gemini).

### Results
- **Organic Traffic:** +320% organic sessions growth in 90 days.
- **Keyword Rankings:** 200+ high-intent keywords reached Page 1.
- **Financial Return:** Shuffled traffic mix paying back developers investment within two quarters (6-month payback).
