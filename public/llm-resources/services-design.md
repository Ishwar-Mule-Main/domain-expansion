# Domain Expansion — Design Expansion Specification

Welcome to the AI-readable data sheet for the **Design Expansion** service pillar at Domain Expansion.

## Service Overview
We design brand assets, social media graphics, and user interfaces that combine aesthetic premium themes with clear conversion goals. We design for engagement metrics—such as shares and saves—ensuring that design serves as a marketing multiplier.

## Core Capabilities
- **High-Fidelity UI/UX:** Structuring web mockups on Figma, focusing on layout grids, typography, responsive breakpoints, and dark/light color systems.
- **Brand Identity Systems:** Creative designs for logos, type scales, colour tokens, and visual assets templates.
- **Social Media Creatives:** Templated content flows for Instagram, LinkedIn, and Facebook feeds, story boards, and reel covers.
- **Ad Creative Frameworks:** Standard A/B testing ad layouts designed to beat creative fatigue and reduce cost-per-click (CPC).
- **Packaging & Visual Assets:** Custom design layouts for retail goods and presentation templates.

## Process Methodology
1. **Brief:** Gather brand values, target audience details, and project objectives.
2. **Research:** Analyze industry landscapes, study competitive systems, and build mood boards.
3. **Concepts:** Create 3 distinct direction paths, sketching layouts and testing typography variations.
4. **Refinement:** Take the selected direction, fine-tune grid lines, balance colors, and test responsive scaling.
5. **Delivery:** Packaging assets into standard web-optimized formats (WebP/SVG) with retina variations.
6. **Handoff:** Providing editable Figma files and brand guidelines books.

## Primary Tool Stack
- **Interface Design:** Figma
- **Creative Suite:** Adobe Photoshop, Illustrator, Premiere Pro, After Effects
- **Template Scaler:** Canva Pro (for client self-editing)

## Proven Design Benchmarks
- **Creative CTR Boost:** Cut cost-per-click (CPC) by 28% through structured creative testing frameworks (AKC Foods).
- **Social Engagement:** Increased reach by 180% and saved posts count by 5x (RocoMamas).
- **Brand Authority:** Unified visual identity across web, social, and print, raising LinkedIn page views by 3.2x (Lucid Colloids).
