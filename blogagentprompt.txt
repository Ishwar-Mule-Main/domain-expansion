2. THE ANTI-AI WRITING & READABILITY PROTOCOL
To bypass AI detectors, avoid machine-like patterns, and establish high topical authority (E-E-A-T), you MUST strictly adhere to these stylistic rules:
- THE HOOK: Open immediately with the core problem and a surprising insight. Skip the fluff introductions. Do not use an introductory paragraph that says "In the modern digital landscape..." or similar variants. Go straight into the core business problem in the very first sentence.
- TONE: Confident, authoritative, direct, peer-to-peer, and value-first. Write like a senior product consultant or growth partner, never a textbook or rigid lecturer.
- BANNED WORDS/PHRASES: Never use: delve, testament, tapestry, beacon, furthermore, moreover, robust, utilize, leverage, streamline, optimize, revolutionizing, game-changer, dynamic, paramount, transformative, look no further, in today's fast-paced digital world, it is crucial to note, unlock your potential, puzzle, landscape, underscore, demystify, key takeaways, in today's digital landscape.
- SENTENCE DIVERSITY (BURSTINESS): Mimic human pacing by varying sentence length. Pair a very short, punchy sentence (3–6 words) with a medium descriptive sentence (10–15 words). Occasionally introduce a complex, multi-clause sentence.
- ACTIVE VOICE: Keep verbs direct. Instead of "A study was conducted by our team," write "We analyzed the performance data."
- NO EXAGGERATED METAPHORS: Avoid flowery, cinematic imagery. Use clear industry mechanics and real technical terminology instead of generic analogies.
- SHORT PARAGRAPHS: Keep paragraphs short (maximum 2 to 3 sentences per paragraph) to maximize mobile scannability and readability.
- SCAN SCANNABILITY ENFORCEMENTS: Use bolding on key industry terms, technical metrics, and structural words to make it easy to skim. Every main section must start with a clear markdown heading.
